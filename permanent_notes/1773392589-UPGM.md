## TCS-107 & TCS-108: Emailing on the 21st and 27th — PR #6337 (merged)
## TCS-109: Autosubmit for early odo submitters — PR #6349

### Context
- Ideally, customers submit odometer reading at the end of the month every
  month - accurate charges
- Help, remind them if they haven't submitted (21st & 27th)
- Auto as backup

### Problem
- Customer submits late
- No reminder is sent
- No auto
- Very early readings are used for fees
- Min charge, poor customer experience, unrealised revenue

### Solution
- Reminders are sent to anyone who hasn't submitted in the last 8 days, even if
  they submitted earlier in the month
- If they STILL forget, we're still stuck with poor autosubmissions
- If there's an early submission, we generate an auto

- Customers submit odo reading end of month
- Uhoh, they did it late - early next month
- Currently, 
- Customers who submitted their odometer reading late (in the following month) were being silently dropped from the reminder email query
- Fixed the query so anyone who hasn't submitted in the last 8 days gets contacted on the 21st and 27th, regardless of when their last submission was
- Both issues tackled in one PR — fixing the 21st query affected the 27th, so they were combined

## Charge Scheme Activities for EVS — PR #6389 (merged)
- The post-delivery check-in activity was firing ~6 weeks after delivery, which didn't line up with when customers need to submit mileage or use public chargers
- Activity is now scheduled for the 25th of the month after delivery, in sync with mileage submission cycles
- Renamed to "Mileage Submission / Public Charge Reminder" and updated with a reminder note to make the action clear to customers

- Customers submitting their odometer reading early (on or before the 18th) were causing inaccurate month-end mileage totals — no auto submission was being generated for the rest of the month
- Updated the autosubmission logic to generate a month-end auto reading for these customers
- Customers who submitted on the 19th or later, or already have an auto submission this month, are left alone
