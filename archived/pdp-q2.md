# PDP Q2

## Goals

### Product-facing Demos
- Present engaging demos to non-technical people to concisely convey work done and
impact
- It feels purposeful and waffle-free, appearing prepared
- Questions/statements are relevant and non-contradictory/confirmational

### Ruthless Prioritization
- When planning work, determine what is the minimum viable value to deliver
  in order to get feedback from internal/external customers

### Ownership
- You write, you release it, you own it
- Handle appsignal errors, good job failures and zendesk support that relate to my features
- (Stretch) Support/handle wider team failure/errors/ZD-support.
